    What the plan page doesn't mention

    It isn't the speed. It's the terms.

    Fixed wireless is a real product and a genuine improvement over what a lot of houses had before. It just comes with conditions that nobody reads out loud.

    Priority

    You're behind every phone on the tower

    Home internet rides the same airwaves as the phones in every car and pocket nearby, and it yields to them by design. That's not a fault — it's the deal. It just means your evening gets quieter as everyone else's gets busier.

    Drift

    It changes when the trees come back

    A signal through the air is a signal through whatever is in the air. Leaves in May, a wet spring, a new building on the lot behind you. The box that worked in February can quietly stop working in June, and nothing on your end changed.

    Neighbors

    It gets slower as it gets popular

    Every house on your street that signs up shares the same tower you do. You feel the success of the product you bought. A line in the ground doesn't work that way — what we built for your house stays yours.
