    No other product does this. A good restaurant getting popular doesn't make your dinner worse. Here, every ad that works on your street is a small deduction from your evening — and there's nothing you can do about it from your side.

    There's no notice, no letter, no line on the bill. One month the call is fine and a few months later it isn't, and the only thing that changed happened in somebody else's living room. Most people blame their own router and go buy a new one.
